Hello! Welcome to my Unreal Engine 5 project! This is a Cancer themed game that is in making. Its chosen theme is one of the themes studied at Norwich Research Park which is Medical Research. Its aim is to teach young undergraduates who are insterested in the medical field. Specifically about Cancer, how it behaves and how the whole body reacts to it. While taking picture and identifying cancer cells, you get to navigate as a Nanobot in different parts of the body discovering cells in your body act and solving knowledge-based puzzles. My project has one level, simple dialogue with one NPC, moving objects, a door that opens, moving NPCs and a take picture mechanic which works but the images aren't being saved to the gallery yet. I will continue to work on it and I am open to any suggestions so I can improve.
